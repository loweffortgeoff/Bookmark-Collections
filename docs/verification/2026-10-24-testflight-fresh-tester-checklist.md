# Tucked — first-time tester checklist

Thank you for testing Tucked. Tucked saves links, images, and short text snippets, and keeps them in sync on your iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

You get **10 items free**. After that, Tucked asks you to buy **Tucked for Life**. In TestFlight, this purchase is free. You are not charged.

Use this checklist only if you have **never** used or bought Tucked before.

## Before you start

1. Install **TestFlight** from the App Store.
2. Open the TestFlight invite, then install **Tucked**.
3. Use Tucked on one iPhone first. If you also have a Mac or an iPad, keep it nearby for step 5.

## 1. Save things

Save 10 items in total. Use a mix:

- **Links:** In Safari, open any web page. Tap **Share**, then **Save to Tucked**, then **Save**.
- **Images:** In Photos, open a photo. Tap **Share**, then **Save to Tucked**, then **Save**.
- **Snippets:** In Tucked, tap **+**, then **New Snippet**. Type a sentence, then tap **Save**.

Check: each item shows as a card in Tucked.

## 2. Save the same link again

1. In Safari, share a link you already saved.
2. Before you save, put it in a collection or turn on **Favorite**, if you like.
3. Tap **Save**.

Check: Tucked still shows **one** card for that link, not two.

## 3. Reach the free limit

With 10 items saved, try to add an 11th item in the Tucked app.

Check:

- A screen appears: **"Ten Bookmarks Is Not Enough"**.
- You can close it. Your 10 items are still there.

Now try the 11th item from Safari's **Share** button.

Check: Tucked tells you it cannot save more items yet. It does not save the item.

**On a Mac only:** the Share message shows **Open Tucked** and **Cancel**. Click **Open Tucked**. Check: Tucked opens.

Last, delete one item, then add a new one. Check: the new item saves without the limit screen. Add one more item so you are back at the limit.

## 4. Buy Tucked for Life (free in TestFlight)

1. In Tucked, try to add an 11th item again.
2. On the "Ten Bookmarks Is Not Enough" screen, tap **Unlock Tucked for Life**.
3. Confirm the TestFlight purchase.

Check:

- The screen closes by itself.
- You can now save an 11th item.
- **Settings** shows **Tucked for Life**.

## 5. Second device (iPad or Mac)

1. Install Tucked from TestFlight on your second device. Use the **same Apple Account**.
2. Open Tucked.

Check:

- Your items appear.
- If Tucked still asks you to buy, tap **Restore Purchases**. Check: Tucked unlocks.

## How to report

For anything that looks wrong, send:

- Your device (for example, iPhone 15 or MacBook Air) and its iOS or macOS version.
- The step number.
- What you expected, and what happened.
- A screenshot, if you can.

In TestFlight, you can also take a screenshot and tap **Share Beta Feedback**.
